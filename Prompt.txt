You are an autonomous senior full-stack engineer, AI engineer, product architect, and UI/UX engineer.

Your task is to independently design and build a complete working prototype for a Razorpay buildathon challenge:

TRACK 03 — AI REVENUE RECOVERY

Goal:
Build an AI agent that detects revenue at risk, determines the appropriate intervention, and executes a bounded recovery workflow.

The system must handle revenue leakage scenarios such as:
- Payment failures
- Checkout abandonment
- Failed subscriptions
- Overdue B2B receivables
- Mandate/payment retry failures
- Promise-to-pay tracking

The core principle is:

    Detect → Diagnose → Decide → Act → Verify → Stop → Audit

The solution must NOT merely identify problems. It must demonstrate actual/measurable money recovered across a batch of at-risk transactions/customers.

The final product should feel like a polished production-grade fintech AI operations platform, while using simulated/mock payment data and integrations for the buildathon.

3. SUPPORTED REVENUE-RISK SCENARIOS
==================================================

Implement at least these six scenarios:

A. PAYMENT DEGRADATION

A customer's previously successful payment behavior starts degrading.

Example:

Customer:
ACME Pvt Ltd

Historical:
95% payment success

Recent:
60% payment success

Failure pattern:
- bank decline
- insufficient funds
- network failures
- repeated retries

Agent must:
- detect degradation
- identify likely root cause
- determine appropriate retry strategy
- execute bounded retries
- stop after the configured retry limit
- escalate when appropriate
- record all actions

--------------------------------------------------

B. CHECKOUT DROP-OFF RECOVERY

A user reaches checkout but does not complete payment.

Data should include:
- checkout started
- amount
- timestamp
- payment method
- device
- geography
- previous customer behavior
- cart/order information

Agent should determine likely cause using available signals.

Possible interventions:
- payment retry link
- alternative payment method
- reminder
- personalized message
- limited-time recovery prompt

Do NOT repeatedly spam the customer.

Implement frequency limits.

--------------------------------------------------

C. FAILED SUBSCRIPTION RECOVERY

A recurring subscription payment fails.

Examples:
- card expired
- insufficient funds
- bank decline
- mandate failure
- payment method unavailable

Agent should:
- diagnose failure
- determine whether retry is appropriate
- schedule retry
- suggest/update payment method when appropriate
- stop after maximum attempts
- escalate if recovery fails

--------------------------------------------------

D. B2B RECEIVABLES CHASER

An invoice becomes overdue.

Data:
- customer
- invoice amount
- due date
- days overdue
- historical payment behavior
- previous promises
- customer segment
- account owner

Agent should classify:

LOW RISK
→ automated reminder

MEDIUM RISK
→ personalized follow-up

HIGH RISK
→ escalation to account manager

CRITICAL
→ management/manual review

The agent must not endlessly chase customers.

Implement stopping rules.

--------------------------------------------------

E. MANDATE RETRY SEQUENCER

Recurring payment/mandate fails.

Agent should select a retry sequence.

Example:

Attempt 1
↓
wait
↓
Attempt 2
↓
wait
↓
Attempt 3
↓
alternate payment method
↓
customer notification
↓
stop/escalate

The retry schedule should depend on failure type.

For example:
- temporary network failure → retry soon
- insufficient funds → retry later
- invalid payment method → request payment method update
- permanent decline → stop retries

--------------------------------------------------

F. PROMISE-TO-PAY TRACKER

For overdue invoices, a customer may promise to pay on a certain date.

Store:
- promise date
- promised amount
- actual payment
- reminder history
- missed promise count

Agent should:

Promise created
↓
Track due date
↓
Payment received?
    YES → mark recovered
    NO → trigger next intervention
↓
Repeated missed promise?
    YES → escalate

13. REVENUE CALCULATIONS
==================================================

Track money precisely.

Definitions:

total_revenue_at_risk
=
sum(amount_at_risk for eligible unresolved cases)

recovered_revenue
=
sum(actual recovered amount)

recovery_rate
=
recovered_revenue / total_revenue_at_risk * 100

Do not count a payment as recovered unless the simulated payment outcome becomes successful.

Do not double-count revenue.

If a ₹10,000 case is recovered:

at risk = ₹10,000
recovered = ₹10,000

If ₹4,000 is recovered:

at risk = ₹10,000
recovered = ₹4,000

If the case is permanently lost:

recovered = ₹0
